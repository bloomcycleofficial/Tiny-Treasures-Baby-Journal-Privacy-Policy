Privacy Policy

Tiny Treasures: Baby Journal

Effective date: November 2026

Last updated: November 2026

In short
Your journal (photos, videos, voice notes, stories, milestones, letters and your child’s details) is stored only on your phone. We never upload it to our servers, and we cannot see it.
We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.
To deliver occasional notifications and seasonal offers, the app uses Google Firebase, which handles a random app-installation identifier and basic device information (such as app version and country/language setting). No journal content is ever included.
Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details.
Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.

1. Who we are

Tiny Treasures (“the app”) is a proprietary application. For any privacy question or request, contact us at bloomcycleofficial@gmail.com.

This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, who it is shared with and the choices you have. It applies to the Tiny Treasures app on Android and to this website.

2. Who the app is for

Tiny Treasures is designed for parents, guardians and other adults to record memories of the children in their care. It is not directed at children, and children should not use it themselves. Although the journal is about children, everything you enter about them stays on your own device (see section 3). We do not collect it.

3. Information that stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is saved in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is not sent to us or to any server operated by us. This includes:

Child profiles: name, nickname and date of birth.
Memories: titles, stories, dates, moods, tags, stickers and monthly growth-photo marks.
Photos and videos you take with the camera or choose from your gallery.
Voice notes you record.
Milestones and their notes, photos, videos and voice notes.
Letters to the future, including any photo and “open on” date.
App settings: reminder times, which tips you have seen, your plan status, the start date of your free trial and similar preferences.

Because this information never leaves your phone through us, we cannot view, recover or restore it for you. Please make regular backups (section 6).

4. Information processed by third-party services

The app uses a small number of Google services to work. Each is listed below with exactly what it handles.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)

Why: to deliver occasional push notifications, such as seasonal greetings, tips and subscription offers.
What it handles: a Firebase installation ID and a push-notification token (random identifiers for this installation of the app, not linked to your name or email), plus basic technical data needed to deliver messages.

Firebase Remote Config (Google)

Why: to fetch the in-app banners and offers we publish, so they can change without an app update.
What it handles: the Firebase installation ID, app version, operating system, and your device’s language and country setting. The country is used only to show campaigns relevant to your region. It comes from your phone’s language/region setting, not from GPS or location services.

Google Play Billing

Why: to sell and manage Premium subscriptions.
What it handles: Google processes the purchase. The app receives only the purchase status (for example “active” or “expired”) so it can unlock Premium. We never receive your payment card, bank details or billing address.

Google Play In-App Review

Why: used only if you choose “Rate the app”.
What it handles: it opens Google Play’s rating screen. Any review you write is handled by Google under its own terms.

Google Fonts

Why: to display the app’s handwritten-style fonts.
What it handles: the first time the app runs, it may download font files from Google’s servers. As with any web request, this exposes your device’s IP address to Google. No other information is sent.

Google’s handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in Privacy and Security in Firebase (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Google acts as our service provider (data processor) for Firebase.

No journal content (no photos, videos, voice notes, text or child details) is ever sent to any of these services.

We do not show advertising and do not include any advertising SDK.
We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
We do not collect your location, contacts, call logs or browsing activity.
We do not sell, rent or trade any information, and do not share it for advertising.
We do not use your information to train artificial-intelligence models.
6. Backups, sharing and exports, under your control

Backup files

The app can create a backup file (.ttb) containing your whole journal. You choose where it goes: save it to a folder on your phone, or share it to an app of your choice (such as Gmail, Google Drive or a messaging app). Once you send or save it, that copy is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy. We never receive it.

Backup files are not encrypted. Anyone who has the file and the Tiny Treasures app can open it. Please store and share it as carefully as you would a photo album.

Restoring

When you restore, you pick a backup file and the app reads it on your phone. While restoring, Android briefly copies the file into the app’s private storage. That temporary copy is deleted afterwards.

PDF memory book and sharing

Exporting a PDF or sharing a memory creates the file on your phone and passes it to the app you choose. We do not receive a copy.

Android device backup

If you have turned on Google’s device backup in your phone’s settings, Android may include some of the app’s data in your personal Google account backup, subject to Android’s size limits. This is controlled by your phone and your Google account, not by us, and we cannot access it.

7. Device permissions

The app asks for permissions only when you use the feature that needs them. You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone’s settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.

Camera: taking photos and recording videos for memories and milestones.
Microphone: recording voice notes and the sound on videos you record.
Photos and videos: choosing existing photos and videos from your gallery. Only the items you pick are copied into the app.
Files: saving a backup to a folder you choose, and choosing a backup file to restore.
Notifications: daily memory reminders, backup reminders, letter “ready to open” reminders, trial reminders, and occasional greetings and offers.
Run at start-up: re-scheduling your reminders after your phone restarts or the app updates.
Internet: notifications, banners and offers (section 4), purchases, and downloading fonts. Your journal never uses the internet.
8. Notifications

Reminders you turn on (daily memory nudge, weekly backup reminder, letter reminders) are created and scheduled entirely on your phone. Greetings, tips and offers may be scheduled by the app from campaigns it downloads, or sent as push messages through Firebase. You can turn off any notifications, or individual notification categories, at any time in your phone’s settings under Apps › Tiny Treasures › Notifications.

9. Free trial and subscriptions

New installations receive a 14-day free trial of Premium. The trial start date is stored only on your phone and is used solely to know when the trial ends. No payment details are needed for the trial. When the trial ends you move to the Free plan, and nothing you have added is deleted.

Subscriptions are purchased, renewed, cancelled and refunded through Google Play. To manage or cancel a subscription, open the Google Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription.

10. Contacting us

If you email us (for example using “Send feedback” in the app), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to improve the app, keep it no longer than needed for that purpose, and never add you to marketing lists without your permission. Please do not send us photos or personal details of your child unless they are needed to resolve your request.

11. Data retention and deletion
Journal and settings: kept on your phone until you delete them in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Any of these removes them permanently. We hold no copy.
Backup files: kept wherever you saved or sent them, until you delete them there.
Firebase identifiers: retained by Google according to its Firebase retention policies. They stop being used when you uninstall the app.
Emails to us: deleted when no longer needed to answer you, or on request.

Because the app has no accounts, there is no account to delete. To erase all app data, uninstall the app or go to Settings › Apps › Tiny Treasures › Storage › Clear data.

12. Security

Your journal is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read, and is protected by your phone’s own encryption and screen lock. Information exchanged with Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your phone locked, keep backups safe, and make backups regularly. If your phone is lost or reset without a backup, your journal cannot be recovered.
